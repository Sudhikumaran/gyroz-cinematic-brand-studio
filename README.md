# GYROZ STUDIO — Official Creative Agency Website

Design a premium, visually immersive, multi-section website for GYROZ STUDIO, a creative and digital marketing agency based in Tiruppur, Tamil Nadu, India.

This is the official business website used to attract brands, businesses, entrepreneurs, creators, and companies looking for professional creative and digital marketing services.

Use the two attached GYROZ STUDIO promotional images as visual references for the brand identity, studio environment, lighting, and aesthetic.

IMPORTANT: Preserve the original GYROZ STUDIO logo and its multicolour design. Do not recreate, distort, recolour, or replace the logo.

---

## 1. Overall Creative Direction

Create a premium creative-agency website with a cinematic, bold, modern aesthetic.

Visual identity:
- Primary background: Deep black (#050806).
- Secondary background: Dark charcoal (#101512).
- Accent: Emerald green (#00E88B).
- Text: White and soft grey.
- Subtle borders: Dark green and graphite.
- Use the original multicolour GYROZ logo for brand recognition.

Design inspiration:
Premium creative production companies, modern digital agencies, cinematic portfolio websites, and high-end design studios.

Visual effects:
- Subtle emerald lighting.
- Cinematic image overlays.
- Elegant scroll animations.
- Large editorial typography.
- Smooth transitions.
- Carefully designed image galleries.
- Premium hover interactions.

Avoid:
- Generic corporate templates.
- Purple gradients.
- Cartoon illustrations.
- Excessive glassmorphism.
- Generic office stock photography.
- Overcrowded layouts.

The website should feel like a real creative production company with a strong visual identity.
